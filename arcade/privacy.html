RIFFCADE

Privacy

Effective 15 October 2026. If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.

RIFFCADE is a collection of open-source browser games at riffcade.com, plus a chat app that brings the arcade into ChatGPT and Claude. It is run by Albert D Matalon, LLC. This page explains what we store, who else is involved, and how to have your data deleted.

What we store

  • Play counts: when a game starts, we store the game, the time, and whether it was played on the website or in a chat app. No ID or other identity is attached.
  • Star ratings: when you rate a game, we store the game, the stars (1 to 5), the time, and a random ID. On the website the ID is created in your browser the first time you rate. It lets a second rating replace your first instead of counting twice. The ID is not linked to your name, email, account or device.
  • Usage counts: on riffcade.com (not in the chat apps) we count steps such as a visit starting, a game starting, today's challenge being played or shared, a "New games every morning" button being tapped (and which wording of the daily prompt you were shown), the privacy choice made, and how regularly the player plays (weak, okay or strong, worked out on your device from the history below). Each count stores the step, the day and a random session ID that your browser creates for the open tab and deletes when the tab closes. No IP address, device details or lasting ID is stored with it.
  • On your device only: your random ID, your last game, your chosen category, your install-prompt choices, the position of the pause button, your daily-challenge streak, a short play history (for each of the last 35 days: how many games and minutes you played and whether you came from your AI's daily task, plus where your first visit came from), the daily-prompt wording you were shown, your privacy choice and the day the "New games every morning" card was last shown are kept in your browser's local storage. A service worker caches the arcade's files so they load faster. None of this is sent to us, except the random ID when you rate a game.
  • Game diagnostics: on riffcade.com (not in the chat apps, never on Kids games) we record how each game ran, so we can find games that break: which game, how long it was played, how quickly it loaded, its frame rate, freezes, error messages, and tapping patterns that suggest a player is stuck (many fast taps in one spot, or lots of taps with no progress). These records carry the same random tab-session ID as the usage counts and the channel you arrived from (for example a Meta ad, your AI's daily list, or direct). Only where analytics is on (below), they also include device details from your browser (phone or computer model, operating system, browser, screen size, language, time zone, connection type) and your country, which our server looks up from your IP address with IPinfo and does not store. Diagnostics are deleted after 30 days.
  • Abuse protection: to limit spam ratings and counts, our server keeps your IP address in memory for one minute and never writes it to the database.

What the chat app receives

ChatGPT and Claude process your conversations under their own privacy policies (OpenAI, Anthropic). RIFFCADE never sees your conversation. It only receives the inputs of the tool the assistant calls:

  • show_arcade: an optional category and an optional search text. They are used to pick games and are not stored.
  • new_games: no inputs.
  • play_game: a game name or slug. It adds one play count as described above. Nothing else is stored.
  • rate_game: the game slug, the stars (1 to 5) and an optional client field. The arcade card in the chat fills client with a random ID that it creates and keeps in the card's local storage. We store it as mcp: followed by that ID, together with the game, the stars and the time, so a repeat rating replaces the first one. If client is missing, we generate a new random ID for that one rating. The ID is not linked to your chat account, your name or your conversation.

Tool requests come from OpenAI's or Anthropic's servers, not from your device.

The AGENTPARK iPhone app

The app shows the same games. It has no account and no sign-in, shows no ads today, and uses no advertising or tracking SDKs. It does not use your advertising identifier and does not track you across other apps or websites.

When the app is first opened it creates a random install ID (12 letters and digits) on your phone. It is used for one thing: to unlock ad-free play after you set up your daily games in ChatGPT or Claude. The ID is part of the message the app opens in your AI, your AI visits our check-in link with it, and the app asks our server whether that ID has checked in. We store the ID, which AI checked in (ChatGPT or Claude) and the time. The ID is not linked to your name, email, Apple ID or device. Deleting the app deletes the ID from your phone; to delete it from our server, email us with the ID shown in the request your AI received.

The games are loaded from agentpark.co. Your 6-minute timer, unlock status and recent games are kept on your phone only.

How long we keep it

Play counts, usage counts and ratings (with their random IDs) are kept for as long as RIFFCADE runs, because they make up the public play and star counts. They are deleted when the service shuts down. The IP addresses used for abuse protection are kept for one minute. Our hosting providers' request logs are kept under their own retention policies.

Deleting your data (AGENTPARK website and Android app)

AGENTPARK (agentpark.co, and the AGENTPARK: Daily Free Games app on Google Play, published by Free Boom Share) has no accounts. To have your data deleted, email privacy@riffcade.com with the subject "Delete my AGENTPARK data" and, if you have it, the random code from the message your AI received (the 10 letters and digits after u= or apu=), or the PayPal email you used for a reward. Within 30 days we delete what is tied to it: your AI's check-ins, your onboarding steps, reward and PayPal records (once any reward is paid) and your notification subscription. Anonymous play counts and star ratings stay, because they hold nothing that identifies you. Clearing the app's storage, or uninstalling it, removes everything stored on your phone.

Deleting your ratings

Email privacy@riffcade.com and we will delete them within 30 days. For ratings made on the website, include your browser ID: . For ratings made in a chat app, tell us the game, the stars and roughly when, and we will find and remove them. Clearing your browser's site data removes everything stored on your device.

Who else is involved

  • GitHub Pages hosts riffcade.com. Railway hosts the server for play counts, ratings and the chat app (mcp.riffcade.com). Cloudflare provides DNS and forwards email sent to privacy@riffcade.com. As with any website, these providers receive your IP address and browser details when you connect, and may log them under their own policies.
  • Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com) serves the typeface on our pages and in the chat card. cdnjs (Cloudflare) serves the three.js library on the home page. Both receive your IP address when your browser loads these files. Our pages set no cookies unless you accept analytics and ad measurement (below).
  • Your choice: outside the United States, the first time you visit we ask whether to allow analytics (Google Analytics) and ad measurement (Meta). Both stay off unless you tap Accept: until then their scripts are not loaded at all and nothing is sent to Google or Meta. In the United States (we go by your device's time zone) they are on by default and we don't show the question; you can turn them off with Privacy choices, and that choice is kept on your device. Change your mind any time with Privacy choices at the bottom of the arcade and the home page; withdrawing stops them and deletes their cookies from our site. Games in our Kids category never load either, whatever you chose. The chat apps, riffcade.com/today and this page load neither.
  • Analytics (only if you accept): Google Analytics 4, provided by Google Ireland Ltd, counts visits, pages and button taps, and for each game: which game, how long you played, your score, how the game ended and where you came from (for example your AI's daily task, and which line of its list you tapped). It also receives the play-history summary from your device (days played in the last week, whether you use the daily task, which AI, where you first came from, whether RIFFCADE is installed), so we can see which habits keep people playing. Google receives your IP address (which it uses for approximate location and does not store), browser and device details, and sets analytics cookies (_ga). Advertising features are off: we don't share this data with Google's ad products. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Meta pixel and Conversions API (only where ad measurement is on): the Meta pixel tells Meta (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd in the EU) that you visited, which game you started (each time you start one) and that you started a second game, and that you tapped a "New games every morning" button. Meta receives your IP address and browser details and may set a cookie (_fbp). Our server also sends Meta the same events (Meta's Conversions API), with your IP address and browser details (passed through, not stored by us), so they are counted when a browser blocks the pixel. If you arrived from one of our ads, we keep Meta's click ID from the ad link together with the random code in your AI prompt, so that when your AI opens AGENTPARK for you (the daily list) or you play a game from it, we can tell Meta the ad worked; that record holds the click ID, the ad's name, your browser's user-agent and dates, and is deleted after 30 days. We use all this only to measure our own ads on Facebook and Instagram. Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Legal basis (EU/UK): analytics and the Meta pixel run on your consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and the ePrivacy rules on cookies). Play counts, ratings and usage counts rely on our legitimate interest in running a free arcade (Art. 6(1)(f)); you can object by emailing us.
  • Ads: the website may offer an optional short video ad you choose to watch (for example, to unlock a bonus level). These are served by Google (AdSense and Google Ad Manager). Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to show and measure ads; outside the US, and until you accept, the ads are non-personalised. You can learn how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites and manage ad personalisation at adssettings.google.com. Nothing is ever forced between or during games, Kids games show no ads, and the chat apps show no ads.
  • The games: every game is served from riffcade.com, and the player blocks links and redirects to other sites. Two open-source games, SkiFree.js and NS-Shaft, include their authors' original Google Analytics code, which may set Google cookies.

Player rewards

If you claim a player reward, we store the PayPal email address you type, the random code from your AI prompt, the amount, the date, a one-way scrambled form of your IP address and of your device type and settings (so one connection or phone can't claim many times), and the country and network name of your connection, and we send your email address and the amount to PayPal (PayPal, Inc.) to pay you, under PayPal's privacy statement. We use the email only to send the reward and stop repeat claims; we never email you for marketing. Email privacy@riffcade.com to have it deleted after the reward is sent.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no names, no location, no chat content. The only email address we keep is the PayPal email of a player who claims a reward (above).
  • We don't sell your data. Google and Meta receive the data described above only if you accept.

Children

RIFFCADE is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from them. Games in our Kids category never load analytics or the Meta pixel. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, email privacy@riffcade.com and we will delete it.

Security

All traffic uses HTTPS. The database holds only game names, stars, times and random IDs, and only the operator can access it on the hosting provider. No system is perfectly secure, but we keep as little as possible so there is little to lose.
