Privacy

RIFFCADE is a collection of open-source browser games. This page explains what the arcade and its chat app (for Claude and ChatGPT) store.

What we store

  • Play counts: when a game starts, we count one play for that game. No identity is attached.
  • Star ratings: when you rate a game, we store the game, the stars, and a random ID created in your browser so a second rating replaces your first instead of counting twice. The ID is not linked to your name, email, account or device.
  • On your device only: your last game, chosen category and install-prompt choices are kept in your browser's local storage and never sent to us.

What we don't do

  • No accounts, no names, no email addresses, no location, no chat content.
  • The chat app receives only the tool requests it needs (which game, which category, a rating) and never reads your conversation.
  • We don't sell data.

Ads

The website version may show ads between games. The chat app versions never show ads.

The games

Each game is someone else's open-source work, shown unmodified with its original licence and credit (tap ⓘ in a game). Games run in your browser.

Contact

Questions or removal requests: open an issue on GitHub.
