How to play

Read the clue

JThe clue tells you what to look for.

Find the place

Walk about and look for the thing in the clue.

Hold to dig

Hold the left button. The block cracks, then breaks.

Depth is the answer

Deeper earth is older. A thing only shows up in its own layer.

Use the brush

When something appears, swap to the brush. The pick will break it.

Put earth back

Right click to fill a hole, or to build a step.

Climb out

Face the wall and hold jump. Press F to fly up and look.

See the city

TThe city appears as it stood.

Use the machines

GSieve your dug earth. It finds small things.

About this game

All of it is real

Every object and every note is a real find. Where experts still argue, the game says so.

Nothing was downloaded

Every picture is drawn by the game when it starts. Every sound is made by the game as you play.

The music

Each dig has its own raga. The drums only play while you are digging.

Built with

three.js and Electron, both free and open. No part of Minecraft is used here.
