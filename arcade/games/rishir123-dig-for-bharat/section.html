Every object in this trench is real, and so is the note attached to it. Where archaeologists still disagree — the purpose of the Lothal basin, the reading of the Indus script — the catalogue says so rather than choosing a side. Stratigraphy is the whole argument: nothing here is dated by how it looks, only by the layer it was found in, which is why the trench is cut from the top down and never from the side.
