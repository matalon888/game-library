## Description draft

Token Cart Tycoon is a cozy-chaotic educational strategy game about the first 30 days of an AI startup. Starting with a hot dog cart, one laptop, $500, five subscribers, 8 GB of RAM, and 20 units of daily compute, players read market news, price their subscription, select a model, purchase infrastructure, and allocate founder attention. Each shift turns forecast demand into an operations report covering capacity, latency, revenue, inference costs, churn, trust, and incidents—making the tradeoffs of operating an AI product visible through play.
