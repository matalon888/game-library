# A little more time — 0.3.0

Relaxed pace offers more time to read patterns and steer, with gentler accumulated pressure and more recovery chances. It is intended for anyone who prefers a less demanding pace; it is not calibrated for a particular age or ability.

Relaxed pace is a checkbox on the game-type screen, independent of Wild/Cadence/Afterimage and Storm/Endless. It is off by default and remembered. All simulation runs at 60 percent speed, preserving the timing relationship between steering, rain, forecasting, matching chains, shields and skin arming. This provides roughly 1.67 times as much real time for each decision. Growth additionally slows from 2.8 to 4.2 simulation seconds (seven real seconds), and integrity rises from three to five. A complete storm therefore lasts five real minutes. Clock, survival result and shed recharge text convert simulation time to real seconds.

The goal is more opportunity to notice and act on the same patterns, with less accumulated spatial pressure and more recovery chances. Continuous movement remains; this is not a turn-based puzzle mode. Tutorials inherit the setting and their demonstration schedules slow alongside rain. Twelve record slots separate pace, type and duration; existing six standard records are retained. Standard engine behavior remains covered by the original baseline digest.
