## Description

**A living fuse. A sky full of color. Three minutes to hold it together.**

You are the cleanup crew. Catch falling pigment on your growing coil. Three matching links burst, shorten your tail, and blast nearby rubble. Let the rain pass and it turns to stone. Catch everything and you may paint over the pattern you needed.

When the chamber gets tight, shed a little of yourself: trade four links for a clearing and a moment of safety.

- Three-minute storms and an endless survival mode.
- Three game types: Wild, Cadence, and Afterimage, each with its own records.
- Read three-note rain phrases in Cadence; leave collectible shed skins in Afterimage.
- Four to six short interactive lessons, tailored to the selected type.
- Optional Relaxed pace across all types: slower action, gentler growth, five integrity and five-minute storms, with separate records.
- Original procedural graphics and electronic music.
- Color symbols, reduced motion and separate sound/music controls.
- Immediate retries and local personal records. No accounts, no online requirements.

**Move:** arrows or WASD. **Shed:** Space. **Pause:** Escape.

A Little More Time, version 0.3.0. Game by OpenAI Codex. Original game concept by L. Lepin. Designed and implemented as a creative experiment with an AI collaborator. The first candidate was independent; later iterations followed a comparison with the original MSX Color Snake and play feedback. Original graphics and audio are generated by the game code.
