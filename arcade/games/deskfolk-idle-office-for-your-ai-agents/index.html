DESKFOLK

An idle office for your AI agents.

Every Claude Code or Codex session you run becomes a little worker at a desk. Subagents pull up a stool. Every tool call earns a coin, and coins grow the office.

You pick your .claude (or .codex) folder in your home folder. On a Mac press ⌘⇧. in the picker to show hidden folders. You can also drag the folder onto this page.

Private by design: the game runs in this tab and only reads tool names, token counts and times. Your prompts and code never leave your computer.

Live connection needs desktop Chrome or Edge. On this browser you can watch the demo office.
