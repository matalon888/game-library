## First 2 minutes, v2 (beat by beat)
1. 0-3 s: title card, one tap. Room 1 opens with the laser already locked on a lone goon; nothing to read.
2. 3-8 s: drag, release, bullet, goon falls with hit-stop, flash and a +N pop. Win card with 3 stars in under 10 s. Satisfying and unfailable.
3. 10-30 s: room 2, two goons in line plus a TNT between them: one shot, a bigger bang. First "oh nice".
4. 30-50 s: room 3, a pillar hides the goon and a hostage stands nearby. First room that can be lost; the bank line shows why.
5. 50-90 s: rooms 4-5, ledges and a shoved crate. The win card says "best N bullets", trick shots, chest 12/30 and "Dust Gulch in 8 rooms". Goals sit on the screen.
6. 90-120 s: ropes (room 6). Steady cadence, a new element about every room. The aim drag works reliably, and the lose card ("1 goon left standing", tap to retry) is fast.

## v1 for comparison
Tap a goon and he is shot. No aim, no bank, no skill. Two goons in sun-bleached rooms, score ticks up, nothing new in 2 minutes. Pleasant for 10 s, then it is the same room again.

## What the best app-store game in this genre does that this one still doesn't
The genre leader gives every room a distinct character and a goal that reads as a puzzle at a glance. The kill itself is a mini cutscene: slow-mo follow-cam on the bullet, a ragdoll, a bigger sound for each ricochet, and a "perfect" fanfare that scales with the shot. Its first 2 minutes also change the shape of the problem (a trap, a moving enemy, a boss) rather than the props. Here the bullet is a thin line, there is no audible escalation per bounce, goons stand still, and rooms are static lines-of-sight. The meta (suit, chest, world distance) is only text on a card, not something to see or touch.

## 5 biggest fixes
1. Escalating bounce feedback: pitch-up per ricochet, a trail that gets brighter and larger, a bullet-cam slow-mo on the final kill.
2. Make the near-miss loud on the lose card: replay the shot that missed by the least and show the gap ("0.3 m off"), and add a one-tap auto-retry.
3. Add motion: moving or patrolling goons and a timed element by room 6-8, because every room is currently solved by one static setup.
4. Show the meta visually: a real chest that fills, the suit preview on the card, a daily room badge on the title, not a line of small text.
5. Fix the card text: trick-shot and suit lines wrap awkwardly on 390 px, and the "(3 more -> Forest suit . 2 trick shots)" line is cramped and ambiguous.
