Support

RIFFCADE puts free, open-source browser games inside Claude, ChatGPT and Grok, and on riffcade.com.

Add it to your AI

Go to riffcade.com and tap your AI. The server link is https://mcp.riffcade.com/mcp (no sign-in).

Something broken?

A game won't load, a rating looks wrong, or the arcade doesn't open in your chat: open an issue on GitHub with the game name and the app you used. We read every one.

Remove a game

If you made one of the games and want it removed, open an issue on GitHub. It comes down within 48 hours.
