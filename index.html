Library

How a game gets in

Licence: only MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, CC0, Unlicense (and MIT-0/0BSD). GPL, AGPL, LGPL, MPL, CC-BY-SA, CC-NC and repos with no licence are dropped. READMEs are scanned for separate non-commercial asset licences.

Mobile (0–10): starts from static signals (viewport meta, touch handlers, responsive canvas, download size) and is then corrected by the self-test: Chrome as an iPhone at 390×844, ten taps and a swipe, with every input handler counted. Overflowing pages, JS errors and slow loads lose points.

Quality (0–10): stars, forks, push recency and issue close-rate. "Thin signal" means under 25 stars and almost no issues.

Self-test pass: the page rendered something and the picture changed while being tapped. Games with no live URL are mirrored, unmodified and with their licence file, to matalon888.github.io/game-library.
